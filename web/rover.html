Simulation · a guess, not data

What Jezero may have looked like about 3.5 billion years ago, when it held a lake. Nobody knows what it really looked like.

What is evidence and what is imagined

Evidence: a lake. Jezero held a lake fed by a river; Perseverance found lake sediments and a delta (NASA).

Evidence: the basin. The ground is NASA MOLA elevation data. The water is filled to , just below where it would spill through the eastern outlet valley in that data, covering about . The lake often stood lower than its outlet (study of the outlet valley), so one fixed level is a simplification.

Evidence: carbonates. Orbiters detect carbonate minerals along the crater's inner margin (our data). The pale band at the waterline stands for them.

Imagined: life. No life has been found on Mars. The rounded mounds are invented microbial structures, modelled on the microbialites that grow on Lake Salda's carbonate shore, and drawn far larger than life.

Imagined: the look. The sky, the colour of the water and ground, and the exact shoreline are guesses.
