Lake Erçek: a closed-basin soda lake beside Lake Van, compared with Jezero Crater

Our search scored 157 places against Jezero. Lake Erçek, in eastern Türkiye, ranks 3, just behind Lake Salda, the lake NASA already pairs with Jezero, and Lake Van. Its water is alkaline (pH 9.7) and it has no outlet, the setting in which Salda deposits the carbonates that link it to Jezero. Unlike Salda and Van, Erçek is barely studied. That makes it worth a closer look, and it also means most of what would confirm it is still unknown.

    05What we could not check

      The single most useful next step: sample Erçek's shoreline and test it for carbonate minerals, especially magnesium carbonates like Salda's hydromagnesite. If they are there, Erçek joins Salda as a Jezero analog; if not, it stays a soda lake with the right chemistry and the wrong rocks.
