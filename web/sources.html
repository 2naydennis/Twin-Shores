Every dataset, library, study and image this project uses

Eight NASA datasets and services supply the measured numbers and the terrain. ESA's Copernicus programme supplies a second elevation model and the satellite map. Published studies supply the water, mineral and sediment evidence, each linked below with how closely it was read.

04Published studies and articles

The four judged variables (water pH, mineral deposits, water body, sediment layers) come from these sources. They were found with an AI tool. Each one is marked by how far it was read:

  • read the full text or page was opened and supports the entry
  • opened the source was opened but did not clearly confirm the entry
  • summary only a search-result summary was seen
  • team a team member opened the source and confirmed the entry

06Images

07AI tools

The challenge approach, the choice of Jezero and Lake Salda, the eight variables and the list of places were the team's decisions. AI tools sped up the work:

  • Claude (Anthropic, Claude Code): wrote the pipeline scripts and this website's code, gathered and checked the study sources, and drafted text. Each AI-written file says so in its header, and commits made with AI help carry a Co-Authored-By line.
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI): background reading on Lake Salda and Jezero Crater, and a first list of variables the team chose from.

Every use, dated, is listed in the AI use table in the repository.
